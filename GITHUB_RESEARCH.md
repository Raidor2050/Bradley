# GitHub / Web Research — Cited Sources

This document records the **public web sources** consulted during the Mukhuti
investigation, with URLs and what each was used for. It is the audit trail behind
the `sources` array in `src/lib/data/genealogy.ts`.

## Surname & etymology
- **Forebears — Mukhuti distribution**
  https://forebears.io/surnames/mukhuti
  ~845 people worldwide, ~92% in West Bengal; variants Mukhuty (~231), Mukuti (~588).
- **Wikipedia — Mukhopadhyay (Mukherjee) surname**
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mukhopadhyay
  The title derives from the village of Mukhuti/Mukhati in West Bengal + Sanskrit
  `upadhyaya` (teacher); older village-forms share origin with Mukherjees. Rarhi
  Kulin Brahmin lineage.
- **Behind the Name — Mukhopadhyay (submitted)**
  https://surnames.behindthename.com/name/mukhopadhyay/submitted
- **Wikipedia — Bengali Kayastha**
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengali_Kayastha
  Confirms Mukhuti/Mukherjee is a Brahmin (not Kayastha) surname category.
- **Ancestry.com — Mukhopadhyay name history**
  https://www.ancestry.com/last-name-meaning/mukhopadhyay
- **LocateFamily — Mukhuty individuals (India)**
  https://www.locatefamily.com/M/MUK/MUKHUTY-1.html
  18 named "Mukhuty" people in India — distribution only, not genealogical proof.

## Geography & history
- **Banglapedia — Barisal District**
  https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Barisal_District
  Chandradwip/Bakla; Mughal conquest 1611; Bakerganj 1797 → Barisal; Hindu
  out-migration 1950/1964/1971.
- **Wikipedia — Shillong**
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shillong
  Capital of Assam 1874–1972; large Bengali settler community.
- **The Shillong Times — History of Shillong**
  https://theshillongtimes.com/2019/08/10/history-of-shillong/
  Missions: Welsh Presbyterian (1841), Baptist, Catholic; Bengali presence.
- **Lakutia / Lakhutia Zamindar Bari (Barisal)**
  https://lakhutiaroyfamily.wixsite.com/lakhutia
  The Roy dynasty's zamindari ~8km north of Barisal; "Jomidar Boro Bari" is a
  generic term — no Mukhuti estate documented here.

## Specific claims evaluated (adverse evidence)
- **Wikipedia — Harendra Coomar Mookerjee (H.C. Mookerjee)**
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harendra_Coomar_Mookerjee
  1887–1956; Governor of **West Bengal** (1951–56); no Shillong post.
- **Government of Assam — List of Governors**
  https://gad.assam.gov.in/information-and-services/honble-governors-honble-chief-ministers-and-chief-secretaries-assam
  Official list contains **no Mookerjee/Mukherjee** → "Assam Governor" claim is
  disproven.
- **Wanderlog — Mawkhar Presbyterian Church review ("Wells Mission")**
  https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1751794/mawkhar-presbyterian-church
  Only "Wells Mission" occurrence: an anecdotal local compound with "Pastor
  Welborn" (c.1965–78). Not a documented mission.

## Integrity notes
- Search engines returned **no** public family tree, record, or document tying a
  specific **Mukhuti** family to "Jomidar Boro Bari" Barisal, or linking any
  named relative (Nishi/Neel/Laxmi/Nalini/Omio/etc.) to public records.
- The named individuals are treated as **private, family-known** people; no
  fabrication of public records has been made.

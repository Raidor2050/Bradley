# Identity Resolution — Mukhuti Investigation

Rules applied before any two records may be treated as the same person. Goal:
never merge people merely because their names look alike.

## Comparison dimensions
Name · aliases · spelling variants · transliteration · father · mother · spouse ·
children · age · dates · occupation · employer · residence · institution ·
associated people · chronology · documents · geography.

## Surname family evaluated
- **Mukhuti / Mukuti / Mukhuty / Mukhti** — spelling variants of the same
  village-derived surname; treated as variant spellings, **not** separate people.
- **Mukhuti ↔ Mukherjee / Mukhopadhyay / Mookerjee** — same Brahmin lineage family
  of names, but a surname connection alone is a **leads signal only**. No
  automatic merge was made between any Mukhuti and any Mukherjee individual.

## Merge policy
Two people are merged only with: source-confirmation + father/match dimension +
location + date compatibility. No person in this dataset met that bar beyond the
family-known identities themselves.

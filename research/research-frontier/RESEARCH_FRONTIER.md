# Research Frontier — Where the evidence currently stops

For every stopping point: what was searched, what was found, what remains
unknown, why the branch stopped, and what to investigate next.

## Frontier 1 — Great-grandparent generation (beyond the "-kanta" brothers)
- **Searched:** surname databases, Banglapedia, gazetteers (IA), cemetery indexes (BACSA), FamilySearch Bengal registers.
- **Found:** nothing tying a Mukhuti family to Barisal estates or the named relatives.
- **Unknown:** names and identities of the great-grandparents.
- **Why stopped:** no primary records located; estate/land records for Barisal are
  not freely searchable online.
- **Next:** land records and district gazetteer page-level OCR; family documents.

## Frontier 2 — Horen Mukherjee / "Assam Governor" claim
- **Searched:** Government of Assam governors list; Mookerjee biographies.
- **Found:** no Mookerjee ever governed Assam; H.C. Mookerjee governed West Bengal.
- **Verdict:** `conflicting` — claim as stated is disproven.
- **Next:** original family oral clarification; written family history.

## Frontier 3 — Bashonto Das / "Wells Mission" / Shillong priests
- **Searched:** Shillong mission histories, church directories, The Shillong Times.
- **Found:** no "Wells Mission"; no priest record for Bashonto Das.
- **Verdict:** `unverified`.
- **Next:** church/parish archives in Shillong; BACSA burial indexes.

## Frontier 4 — Das & Mukherjee surname links (Moni Tata engineer; Bhutu SP)
- **Searched:** Tata history publications; police/government directories; newspapers.
- **Found:** no located records.
- **Verdict:** `unverified` candidates.
- **Next:** newspaper digitizations (Google News Archive); PARL/employment records.

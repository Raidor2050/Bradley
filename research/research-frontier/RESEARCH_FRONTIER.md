# Research Frontier — Where the evidence currently stops

For every stopping point: what was searched, what was found, what remains
unknown, why the branch stopped, and what to investigate next. Mirrors
`frontierCandidates` in `src/lib/data/genealogy.ts` (4 frontiers after Round 2).

## Frontier 1 — Great-grandparent generation (beyond the "-kanta" brothers)
- **Searched:** surname databases, Banglapedia, gazetteers (IA), cemetery indexes
  (BACSA), FamilySearch Bengal registers, explicit web searches for each named
  direct-line relative (Nishi/Neel/Laxmi/Nalini Kanta, Bernard Bonoj — nothing found).
- **Found:** nothing tying a Mukhuti family to Barisal Sadar estates or to the
  named relatives.
- **Unknown:** names and identities of the great-grandparents; the actual father
  of Bernard Bonoj Mukhuti.
- **Why stopped:** no primary records located; estate/land records for Barisal are
  not freely searchable online.
- **Next:** land records (DLRMS) and district gazetteer page-level OCR; family documents.

## Frontier 2 — Horen Mukherjee / "Assam Governor" claim
- **Searched:** Government of Assam governors list (colonial + post-1947); Mookerjee
  biographies (H.C. Mookerjee; Syama Prasad Mookerjee; Hiren Mukerjee).
- **Found:** no Mookerjee ever governed Assam. Best interpretation: the tradition
  **fuses** H.C. Mookerjee (real Governor, West Bengal) and Syama Prasad Mookerjee
  (real "Mukherjee + Assam + undivided Bengal", minister, never a governor).
- **Verdict:** `conflicting` — claim as stated is disproven; fusion added as the
  revised interpretation.
- **Next:** original family oral clarification; written family history; Barisal-area
  Mukherjee family records (National Library Kolkata / WB State Archives).

## Frontier 3 — Bashonto Das / "Wells Mission" / Shillong priests
- **Searched:** Shillong mission histories, church directories, The Shillong Times,
  Purple PCI Shillong / Welsh Mission records.
- **Found (Round 2):** "Wells Mission" = phonetic **Welsh Mission** (Presbyterian
  Church of Wales); Mawkhar Presbyterian Church is its mother church; first chapel
  1874; last Welsh missionary left 1969 (matches the anecdotal "Wells Mission
  compound / Pastor Welborn", c.1965–78). No priest named Das located.
- **Verdict:** mission identification `supported`; person `unverified`.
- **Next:** Mawkhar Presbyterian (PCI) and Welsh Mission baptism/membership registers;
  Welsh Mission Hospital records; BACSA burial indexes.

## Frontier 4 — Collateral documented Mukhutis of the Barisal region
- **Searched:** colonial secret-police records (Chopra & Chopra *Who's Who*, 1992),
  US SSA records, ICJ 1965 report.
- **Found (Round 2, documented context):** Hemendra Nath Mukhuti (discharged in the
  Barisal Conspiracy Case, 1913) and Dwijendra Mukhuti (Sonarang National School
  headmaster, Anushilan Samiti); Basanti K Mukhuti + Manindra Nath Dass (Bisharkandi,
  Barisal district; US SSA record). **No document connects any of these to Bradley's
  direct line.**
- **Verdict:** individuals `verified` as context; relationship to the direct line
  unknown (`possible-relative`, confidence 0.05).
- **Next:** DNA testing of documented Mukhuti descendants; Barisal civil/land
  records for Barisal Sadar and Banaripara; archival refugee registers.

## Frontier 5 — Das & Mukherjee surname links (Moni Tata engineer; Bhutu SP)
- **Searched:** Tata history publications; Shillong/Assam police directories;
  newspapers.
- **Found:** no located records (the Shillong SP line is pre-1947 vs post-1972
  ambiguous in the family tradition).
- **Verdict:** `unverified` candidates.
- **Next:** newspaper digitizations (Google News Archive); PARL/employment records;
  Assam/Meghalaya police lists.

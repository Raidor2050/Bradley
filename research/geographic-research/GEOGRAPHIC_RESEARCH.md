# Geographic Research — Mukhuti Investigation

The family geography and what each place does (and does not) establish.

## Barisal Sadar localities (family-provided)
- **Notun Para** — residence per family list (Nishi Kanta branch).
- **Bogura Road** — residence per family list (Neel Kanta branch).
- **Bangla Bazar (eastern side), "Jomidar Boro Bari"** — Bernard Bonoj Mukhuti
  associated here. "Jomidar Boro Bari" is a **generic** zamindar-house term; no
  record ties a specific Barisal estate to a Mukhuti family.
- **Kazipara Sarak** — residence per family list (Nalini Kanta branch).

## Historical context (Barisal)
- Chandradwip/Bakla era; Mughal conquest 1611; Bakerganj district 1797 → Barisal.
- Lakhutia Roy zamindari ~8 km north of Barisal — the only documented estate near
  the localities; unrelated to the Mukhuti family.
- Hindu out-migration waves: 1950, 1964, 1971.

## Shillong (Das family claim)
- Capital of Assam 1874–1972; large Bengali settler community.
- Missions: Welsh Presbyterian (1841), Baptist, Catholic. **No "Wells Mission"
  documented** — the claim is flagged adverse.
- Bashonto Das (priest) and Bhutu Das (SP) have no verified Shillong records.

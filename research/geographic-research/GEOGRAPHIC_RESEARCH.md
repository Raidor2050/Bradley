# Geographic Research — Mukhuti Investigation

The family geography and what each place does (and does not) establish.
Data: `mapLocations` in `src/lib/data/genealogy.ts` (11 locations after Round 2).

## Barisal Sadar localities (family-provided)
- **Notun Para** — residence per family list (Nishi Kanta branch).
- **Bogura Road** — residence per family list (Neel Kanta branch).
- **Bangla Bazar (eastern side), "Jomidar Boro Bari"** — Bernard Bonoj Mukhuti
  associated here. "Jomidar Boro Bari" is a **generic** zamindar-house term; no
  record ties a specific Barisal estate to a Mukhuti family.
- **Kazipara Sarak** — residence per family list (Nalini Kanta branch).

## Barisal-region documented Mukhuti presence (Round 2, collateral)
- **Sonarang / Tangibari (Dacca region)** — base of the documented Mukhuti
  revolutionaries: Dwijendra Mukhuti (headmaster, Sonarang National School) and
  Hemendra Nath Mukhuti (discharged in the Barisal Conspiracy Case, 1913).
- **Bisharkandi (Banaripara thana, Barisal district)** — birthplace (1923) of
  Sudhir Kumar Dass, son of Basanti K Mukhuti and Manindra Nath Dass (US SSA
  record). Confirms Mukhuti families and a Mukhuti–Das marriage in Barisal
  district. These are **context** locations, not confirmed direct-line homes.

## Historical context (Barisal)
- Chandradwip/Bakla era; Mughal conquest 1611; Bakerganj district 1797 → Barisal.
- Lakhutia Roy zamindari ~8 km north of Barisal — the only documented estate near
  the localities; unrelated to the Mukhuti family.
- Hindu out-migration waves: 1950, 1964, 1971 (ICJ 1965 report documents the
  Bakarganj exodus chapter).

## Surname-origin geography
- **Mukhuti/Mukhati village** — West Bengal, the etymological origin of the
  surname (village + *upadhyaya*). A published Brahmin family tree carries a
  "Mukhuti gram" node.
- **Bankura** — "Mukhoti village near Bankura" proposed (per Niharranjan Ray,
  *Bangalir Itihas*) as a possible alternate origin of the Mukhati/Mukhuti variant.

## Shillong (Das family claim)
- Capital of Assam 1874–1972; large Bengali settler community.
- Missions: Welsh Mission = Presbyterian Church of Wales (mother church: Mawkhar
  Presbyterian; first chapel 1874; last missionary left 1969). **"Wells Mission"
  resolved as "Welsh Mission"** (Round 2).
- Bashonto Das (priest) and Bhutu Das (SP) have no verified Shillong records.

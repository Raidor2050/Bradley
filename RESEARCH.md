## 3. The two challenged family claims

### 3.1 "Dr Horen Mukherjee — Assam Governor" → DISPROVEN (as stated)

- The family tradition claims a "Dr Horen Mukherjee" who was Governor of Assam,
  connected to Shillong and undivided Bengal.
- **Documented reality:** The closest real figure is **Harendra Coomar Mookerjee
  (1887–1956)**, Governor of **WEST BENGAL** (1951–56), Vice-President of the
  Constituent Assembly, a Bengali Christian — **never** connected to Assam's
  governorship.
- The **official list of Governors of Assam contains no Mookerjee/Mukherjee** at
  any point.
- **Verdict:** The "Assam Governor" element is ADVERSE. Identity match between the
  family's "Horen" and H.C. Mookerjee is **unresolved** — never merge on name
  similarity alone.

### 3.2 "Wells Mission", Shillong (priest: Bashonto Das) → UNVERIFIED, likely a mis-remembering

- No "Wells Mission" appears in documented Shillong mission history (Welsh
  Presbyterian 1841-, Baptist, Catholic Salvatorian 1890 / Salesian 1922).
- The only occurrence is an anecdotal user recollection of a "Wells Mission
  compound" with a "Pastor Welborn" near Mawkhar Presbyterian Church (c.1965–78).
- **Verdict:** Treat as unverified tradition; pursue church baptism/membership
  records, not public web sources.

## 4. What the research does confirm (context)

- **Barisal Sadar** localities (Bangla Bazar, Bogura Road, Kazipara, Notun Para)
  are all real places. Barisal is the historic town of the Chandradwip/Bakla
  region; a zamindar-dynasty region; large Hindu out-migration after the 1950,
  1964 and 1971 events.
- **Shillong** was the capital of the Chief Commissioner's Province of Assam
  (1874) until 1972; a large Bengali (esp. Sylheti caste-Hindu) settler community
  lived there through the colonial era — consistent with a Das/Mukherjee family
  presence, though not a proof of these specific people.
- The **Mukherjee/Mukhopadhyay lineage** is one of Bengal's great Brahmin
  lineages; village-name+title surnames (Chattopadhyay, Banerjee, Gangopadhyay,
  Mukhopadhyay) are a documented naming system.

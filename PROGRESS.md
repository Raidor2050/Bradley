## The honest answer

- **3 family-known generations** anchor the tree: Bradley Mukhuti (G0) → Bernard
  Bonoj Mukhuti (probable father, G1) → Nishi Kanta Mukhuti (supported
  grandfather, G2).
- The **Mukhuti surname is real and historically deep** (~854 people worldwide,
  plus Mukhuty/Mukhati/Mukhoti variants) — the older village-derived form of the
  Bengali Brahmin **Mukherjee / Mukhopadhyay** lineage.
- **Round 2 public research (documented, collateral context — not direct line):**
  - **Barisal Conspiracy Case (1913):** *Hemendra Nath Mukhuti* (Sonarang,
    Dacca) was "discharged in the Barisal conspiracy case"; *Dwijendra Mukhuti*
    was headmaster of Sonarang National School and an Anushilan Samiti member
    (Chopra & Chopra, *Forgotten Heroes of India's Freedom Struggle*, 1992).
  - **US Social Security record:** *Basanti K Mukhuti* and *Manindra Nath Dass*
    were parents of a son born 1923 at Bisharkandi, **Barisal district** — a
    documented Mukhuti–Das marriage in Barisal, parallel to the family tradition.
  - **"Wells Mission" resolved → "Welsh Mission":** Mawkhar Presbyterian Church
    is the mother church of the Welsh Mission (Presbyterian Church of Wales),
    first chapel 1874, last missionary left 1969.
  - **"Dr Horen Mukherjee — Assam Governor"** stays **conflicting**: no
    Mookerjee ever governed Assam (the tradition is best explained as a fusion
    of H.C. Mookerjee, West Bengal Governor, and Syama Prasad Mookerjee,
    "Mukherjee + Assam + undivided Bengal").
- **0 direct ancestors beyond family knowledge are verified.** The great-grandparent
  generation is the research frontier.
